**We believe in:**

1. **Free is complete** — Core features are never paywalled

Auto Accept uses RevenueCat for subscription management.

### What RevenueCat sees

## Frequently Asked Questions

**A:** No. We commit to keeping core features free forever.

### Q: What if I don't pay for Pro?
**A:** Free tier remains fully functional. No degradation, no nag screens.

### Q: Can I use Pro on multiple machines?
**A:** Yes. RevenueCat subscription covers personal use on all your devices.

**A:** Yes. 50% off Pro for students. Email your `.edu` address to support@autoaccept.dev.
